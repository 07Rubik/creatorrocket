About CreatorRocket

CreatorRocket is an independent set of browser-based tools for brainstorming and planning social content. It is not affiliated with TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or other platforms.

The current tools use local templates, not a live AI service. Your generator inputs are processed in this page. Saved ideas and planner entries use local browser storage on this device. They are not sent to a CreatorRocket server. Clearing browser data may remove saved items, so use Export for a backup.

Do not enter passwords, private messages or sensitive personal information. There is currently no account system, analytics integration, advertising or payment processing.
